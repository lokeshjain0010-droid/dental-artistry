Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions.

I have tooth pain right now. Can I come in today? expand_more

Yes. We keep daily reserved time blocks open specifically for dental emergencies so we can get you out of pain the exact same day you call. Call us directly or request an urgent call below.

What if I’m nervous about dental visits? expand_more

Just let us know on our quick intro call. Dental anxiety is extremely common. We use extra-strength topical numbing gels before any injection, offer noise-canceling headphones with streaming television, and move completely at your pace with hand signals to pause anytime.

How do I book my first visit? expand_more

Simply request a quick call below or call us directly at (415) 555-0199. Our dedicated patient coordinator will answer your questions, explain what to expect, and reserve a convenient morning or afternoon slot.
