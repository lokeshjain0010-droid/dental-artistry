## Brand & Style

The brand persona is an architectural sanctuary for advanced restorative aesthetics and clinical precision. The visual language rejects sterile medical tropes, clinical blues, and synthetic gradients in favor of an understated luxury editorial atmosphere. It balances warm, organic tactility with the exactitude of modern structural architecture.

The intended emotional response is immediate composure, trust, and refined quietude. Interfaces evoke high-end art monographs, bespoke interior architecture ateliers, and calm residential pavilions. The design style combines Architectural Minimalism with Warm Editorial Luxury: expansive negative space, delicate structural dividing rules, deliberate asymmetry, and meticulous typographic pairings.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by the architectural interplay of organic minerals and deep shadow:

- **Primary (`#2C4A3E` - Deep Sage Olive):** Reserved for deliberate points of intention, focal interactive CTAs, verified clinical credentials, and active state indicators. It evokes sculpted marble and botanical courtyards.
- **Secondary (`#141413` - Espresso Charcoal):** Used for foundational typographic dominance, high-impact inverted monograph sections, footers, and crisp keyline accents.
- **Tertiary (`#E6E2D8` - Muted Sand):** Governs structural framing, subtle cell dividers, hair-width borders, and tactile resting states.
- **Neutral (`#F7F5F0` - Warm Alabaster Cream):** The ambient light foundation across all main canvases, replacing sterile clinical whites with restorative warmth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Bone White):** Reserved for elevated clinical cards, appointment orchestration sheets, and floating modal components.

Inverted sections utilize `#141413` as a primary canvas with `#F7F5F0` editorial text and `#2C4A3E` applied as subtle luminescent accents or tonal backdrops. Blue hues, high-saturation alerts, and cold grays are strictly prohibited.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes an architectural dialogue between classical humanism and contemporary structural clarity:

- **Playfair Display (Serif):** Deployed for monumental headline statements, clinical discipline titles, and editorial quotes. Headings should feel sculpted and deliberate, favoring lighter weights (`400` to `500`) with tight negative tracking on larger scales to emphasize editorial rigor.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Sans-Serif):** Delivers clean optical legibility for patient guidance, consultation notes, treatment plans, and technical dental procedures. Its geometric balance maintains clarity without looking utilitarian or robotic.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Rendered exclusively in uppercase with tracking between `+0.08em` and `+0.1em` to evoke architectural blueprint notations and gallery index marks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors modern architectural pavilion design: grounded, rhythmic, and unafraid of empty space.

- **Grid Architecture:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by maximum structural boundaries (`1360px` max-width container). Desktop views utilize wide `4rem` outer borders to isolate UI modules cleanly from browser edges.
- **Rhythm & Proportions:** Vertical flow adheres to an open rhythm where sections breathe through `space-2xl` increments. Negative space is functional: it lowers cognitive strain and instills patient confidence prior to consultations.
- **Breakpoints & Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, wide asymmetric splits (e.g., 5-column editorial narrative against 7-column architectural imagery).
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, gutters shift to `1.5rem`, margin to `2rem`. Complex consultation forms transition to 2-column balanced layouts.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` margins. Multicolumn treatment comparisons reflow cleanly into vertically stacked cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material separation rather than standard SaaS shadows. It is achieved through:

- **Warm Planar Layering:** Pure Bone White (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces float against the Warm Cream (`#F7F5F0`) ambient canvas, defined by hairline perimeter borders (`#E6E2D8`) rather than thick dropshadows.
- **Architectural Ambient Shade:** When interactive or floating dialogs demand elevation (e.g., calendar popovers, aesthetic simulations), use a soft, extra-diffused tint: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(20, 20, 19, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 74, 62, 0.03)`. The shadow carries a trace of deep charcoal and sage olive rather than neutral gray.
- **Low-Contrast Framing:** Internal card dividers and structural separators use `1px solid #E6E2D8`, echoing fine architectural joinery and framing.

## Shapes

The geometry is defined by soft architectural curvature:

- **Primary Geometry (`roundedness: 2`):** Base components, cards, input frames, and modal dialogs use `0.5rem` (8px) corners. Larger structural panels and feature focus zones use `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px) or `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) to balance sharp keylines with hospitable softness.
- **Tactile Transitions:** Avoid harsh pill shapes except for discreet pill tags and clinical status chips, preserving a sophisticated architectural character across primary touchpoints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Sage Olive (`#2C4A3E`) with Warm Cream (`#F7F5F0`) text. Border radius `0.5rem`. Smooth hover transition to `#1F352C` with subtle forward translation.
- **Secondary / Architectural Outlined:** Hairline border (`1px solid #141413`), transparent background, `#141413` text. Hover fills to `#141413` with `#F7F5F0` text.
- **Text Link:** Editorial serif or uppercase sans-serif label underlined with a `1px` rule offset by `4px`, accented with an animated chevron on hover.

### Floating Cards
- Rendered in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) over the Warm Cream (`#F7F5F0`) substrate.
- Framed with `1px solid #E6E2D8`, radius `0.5rem` or `1rem`, and generous internal padding (`space-lg`).
- Used for clinician bios, treatment plans, and 3D intraoral scan previews.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded with `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
- Aesthetic states: `#F7F5F0` background with `1px solid #E6E2D8` and `#2C4A3E` label typography.
- Status indications (e.g., appointment confirmed) use deep olive tints without garish alert colors.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Cream tinted (`#F7F5F0`).
- Border: `1px solid #E6E2D8`. Radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus State: Border color shifts cleanly to `#2C4A3E` with a faint `2px` outer ring in `rgba(44, 74, 62, 0.15)`. No harsh primary blue rings.
- Placeholder text in muted charcoal (`#8C887B`), transitioning to `#141413` on entry.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square (`checkbox`, `0.25rem` radius) or circle (`radio`), framed with `1.5px solid #E6E2D8`.
- Checked state: Filled with `#2C4A3E` featuring a crisp `#FFFFFF` check or inner dot.

### Bespoke Additions (Treatment Timeline & Consultation Drawer)
- **Treatment Stepper:** Vertical sand rule (`#E6E2D8`) connecting clinical phases, with Sage Olive numbered stone markers.
- **Consultation Drawer:** Off-canvas planning panel in Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) bordered with Sand (`#E6E2D8`), sliding from right with an ambient diffused shade.
